What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple calculation using your height and weight to determine your weight category.

  • Underweight: Below 18.5
  • Normal: 18.5 - 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 - 29.9
  • Obese: 30.0 and Above
